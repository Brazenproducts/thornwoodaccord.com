"The fae courts exist in the spaces between what we allow ourselves to believe. I just wrote down what I found there."

Fern Allern writes dark fae romantasy with roots in folklore, old bargains, and the kind of magic that always costs something real. Her stories live in the space where the beautiful and the dangerous are the same thing — where courts of impossible power operate by rules that were old before language, and where humans stumble in carrying only their stubbornness and whatever luck they have left.

The Elvin Trilogy is her debut series. Book One — The Thornwood Accord — is available now as a free audiobook. The full thirty-chapter arc of Book One is in production, with ten chapters released.

Books Two and Three are in progress. The story isn't finished. Neither is the Accord.
